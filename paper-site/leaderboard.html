CTRL · controlled pre-training
Each method trains from scratch with ViT-B/16 on unlabeled ImageNet-1K for 300 epochs. Optimization and augmentations are aligned where compatible, preserving mechanisms such as masking, memory banks and teacher updates. Figure 4 uses the 300-epoch result; intermediate evaluations occur at 100 and 200 epochs.
Interpreting a difference
Architecture, pre-training, feature extraction and downstream evaluation can all change. The difference measures their combined effect. The shared recipe may suit some methods better than others.

How to read the scores

LP denotes linear or frozen-backbone probing with a task-specific head. AP denotes attentive probing. Each setting uses one seed. Subsets and related tasks count separately; a win count does not measure statistical significance.

The paper plots percentages divided by 100 and errors mapped to 1/(1 + error), then scales each five-model group to its own maximum separately for LP and AP. This table preserves the source scores before normalization. SigLIP 2-G is the vision-language reference. The plotting source labels the RAEv2 series RAEv2-K7; exact feature-to-run correspondence remains unverified.
