Image recognition tells only part of the story.

For NYUv2 depth, adding local patches to whole-image agreement raises held-out rank correlation from 0.01 to 0.67. On ImageNet-1K it stays at 0.88. Keeping both tasks exposes a difference a recognition-only leaderboard would miss.
