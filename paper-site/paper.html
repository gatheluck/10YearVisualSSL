Image recognition tells only part of the story.

Consider depth. Agreement between whole-image features predicts little of the ordering on NYUv2. Add agreement between local patches and the held-out rank correlation rises from 0.01 to 0.67. On ImageNet-1K it stays at 0.88. Keeping both tasks exposes a difference a recognition-only leaderboard would miss.
